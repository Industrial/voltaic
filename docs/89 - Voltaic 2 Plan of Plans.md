# Voltaic 2 — Plan of Plans

Status: active index
Tracks which plans, taken together, implement `docs/86 - Voltaic 2.md` as detailed by
`docs/87 - Voltaic 2 Surface Design.md` and `docs/88 - Voltaic 2 Blueprint.md`.

**This document is an index only.** It does not author plan content — each row becomes
its own plan document, `docs/<doc> - <plan> - <Name>.md`. Update the **Status** column
here as each plan is authored, executed and shipped; do not let plan detail leak back
into this file.

The three habits from `docs/04 - Voltaic Plan of Plans.md` are kept, and the second
and third are what this register exists to honour across a generation change:

1. **Index only.** Scope is one cell, not a section.
2. **Correct stale labels in place**, with a date and the evidence that corrected them.
3. **Never delete a superseded row.** Mark it `superseded` and name what replaced it.

`docs/04` is **not** superseded by this file. It remains the register for plans 01–91
and is the authority on what generation 1 did. This register starts at 92 and the two
never renumber each other.

## Milestone 13 — the reversal

One row, and it is the only row in this register that changes a decision rather than
building something.

| # | Plan | Scope | Status | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 92 | The Charter Reversal | Reverse doc 03 §2 clause 1 and doc 02 §12.4. Add clause 5. Amend `AGENTS.md`. Record the acceptance of the three normative documents. **No code.** | `done` 2026-11-14 | — |

Row 92 landed with two corrections to its own plan, both in doc 90 §10: §2.1 listed
**seven** citations of the constraint and the tree held **twelve** — the gate found five
the reconnaissance missed, including the published crate README and the book — and §6
claimed `STATUS.md` was untouched, which a *generated* document cannot be. The second is
a distinction worth carrying forward to row 104: a hand-written gated document cannot be
written ahead of its code, a generated one must be regenerated by every commit that
moves a number it measures.

"**No code**" also turned out to be approximate. The row ships one new gate,
`no_living_document_asserts_the_retired_charter`, because the failure mode of a
documentation reversal is a half-applied one and a checklist cannot catch that.

**Why it is a plan and not a paragraph.** The constraint being reversed is cited by
`AGENTS.md`, doc 01 §25.1, doc 02 §6.2 and §12.4, doc 03 §2 and §22, `DECLINED.md`,
and the *What this milestone does not claim* section of doc 04. A reversal that edits
one of those and not the others leaves the repository contradicting itself, and the
repository's own convention is that a correction is recorded with its evidence rather
than applied silently. Plan 92 is that record.

Leaves 1 and 2 shipped: the closed alphabet, the subset lattice, and upward
propagation. `voltaic sig` now prints rows, and `announce : () ! async + io` on this
repository's own `examples/async.vlt` is an inferred **join** — the case a

Two notes for later rows, from doc 93 §10:

- **`infer_row.vlt` is also row 99's answer key.** It reads the effects generation 1
  marks *explicitly* — `async`, a `Result`/`Option` return type, a printing macro,
  `:=`, `try`, `catch` — so when row 99 deletes those markers, this corpus is what
  says whether the row preserved the same fact. Exactly the relationship plan 94 §1.4
  set up between generation 1's signatures and inferred types.
- **Leaf 3 cannot move before row 99.** `else`, `.must` and `~` are generation 2
  surface. The discharge *mechanism* exists and is already used by the
  `try`/`catch`/`??` arms, so the engine is ready for the spelling before the
  spelling is.
| 96 | Mode Inference | The three-element chain, use-site constraints, the least-fixed-point solve, lifetime emission, `@` ascription, `VLT0601`. | `in-progress` — leaves 1, 2, 3, 6 `done` 2026-11-14 | 94, 95 |

**Criterion 1 is met.** With every annotation deleted from consideration, inference
reproduces **all fourteen** of `cursor.vlt`'s hand-written receiver modes — four
`&mut self` and eight `&self`, on a target chosen because its annotations are not
uniform. Doc 94 §10 has four findings, and two change later rows:

- **A receiver's mode needs the call graph** (§10.3). `eat` is `&mut self` only
  because it calls `.bump self`; nothing in its own body mutates. That is doc 88 §3's
  whole-crate-solve claim made real, and the per-file driver carries the fixed point.
- **The oracle is sharp for receivers and blunt for by-value parameters** (§10.4).
  Four parameters disagree and inference is right about all four: the type is `Copy`,
  so the author's choice was free. Separating "free choice" from "forced choice" needs
  to know whether a type is `Copy` — **the same declaration environment `Member` is
  waiting on in row 98.**

Leaves 4 and 5 are blocked on the surface, like row 95's leaf 3: `@clone` cannot be
written until row 99 admits it, and no inferred borrow can be emitted until the
emitter consumes the judgement. The engine is ahead of the spelling, deliberately —
measured against the answer key while the answer key still exists.
| 97 | Refinements | Predicate extraction, the QF_ALIA fragment, the solver interface and pin, the timeout-is-a-diagnostic policy, `tests/refine/`. | `in-progress` — leaf 1 `done` 2026-11-14 | 94 |

Leaf 1 shipped, and it makes the refusal **structural** rather than checked (doc 95
§10.1): a `Term` is a map from names to integer coefficients, so a product of two
variables cannot be built at all. That is §1.4's "a timeout is never a pass" in its
strongest form — the undecidable case has no representation. `i * j` is refused
naming the rule and the subterm before any solve runs, which is §3's fourth target and
the one the plan calls more important than the three positive cases.

**Leaf 2 is blocked by its own criterion**, not for want of effort (doc 95 §10.2).
§2.1 makes the solver a dependency rather than an implementation, and §4.1 says the
pin is recorded *with its measured startup cost, because a pin without a measurement
is a guess*. That measurement needs a vendorable offline solver, which CI's
no-build-time-network constraint makes a toolchain decision this leaf owns.
`docs/COMPILER.md` §6 already carries the requirement list a candidate must satisfy.

**Milestone test:** `tests/solve/` green with an expectation file for every corpus
program, and `voltaic-bench`'s three new axes inside the thresholds doc 88 §4.2
states. A solver with no golden corpus is the one part of this generation that could
regress invisibly.

### Why 104 is last, and why that is a finding rather than scheduling

`GRAMMAR.md`, `DECLINED.md`, `CONFORMANCE.md` and `STATUS.md` are **gated**. Every
`GRAMMAR.md` row carries evidence that `crates/voltaic-cli/tests/grammar_inventory.rs`
resolves; `declined.rs` asserts every doc 03 §22 construct appears in `DECLINED.md`;
`status.rs` generates `STATUS.md` from the tree and fails on drift.

So **a gated reference document cannot be written ahead of the code it grades.**
Writing the new `GRAMMAR.md` at the start of this generation would produce a document
whose every evidence path points at a file that does not exist yet, and whose
`UNREACHABLE` fixtures cannot be asserted to fail because they cannot be asserted at
all. The reference docs are an *output* of the turn.

This is also why doc 86 §10's table of closing gaps is labelled a prediction. Plan 46
measured that of sixteen apparent gaps in a careless first pass, **eleven were the
probe's own bug**. A predicted grade is worth what a prediction is worth.

## What this register does not claim

- **No dates.** Doc 04's posture, kept: when a row lands is not a measurement.
- **No row count as progress.** Row 99 is larger than 92, 93, 94 and 98 together.
- **No claim that generation 2 is better on every axis.** Doc 86 §5.6 records two
  regressions — general monadic comprehension over user types, and nested optionals —
  and doc 87 §22.2 marks the first a debt rather than a decision. A register that only
  counted gains would hide them.
- **No claim that the risks in doc 88 §6 are solved.** Three of them cannot be
  measured until there is an implementation, and the thresholds in doc 88 §4.2 exist
  so that the design can be found wrong rather than defended.
